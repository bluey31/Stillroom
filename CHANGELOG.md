## [1.0.0] — 2026-05

### Added
- Initial release. Warm dark theme calibrated for Python and the apothecary side of the terminal.
- Active syntax dyes: lichen, ecru, petal, woad, glaze, eucalyptus, ember, madder, buff.
- Twelve-stop neutral ramp on the H345° warm-ink axis.
- Cross-tool ports: Zed, Ghostty, Slack, Linear, Chrome.
